## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. Vehicle Rental Operations Analytics
**Python · SQL · Pandas · NumPy · MySQL · Tableau**

Analyzed vehicle rental operations to understand customer demand,
vehicle utilization, charging performance, and operational efficiency.

- Analyzed **45,701 rental records** across 52 vehicles.
- Investigated hourly and weekday rental demand patterns.
- Evaluated customer pickup behavior in relation to vehicle battery levels.
- Compared customer rentals with service-related rentals.
- Identified operational improvement opportunities using data-driven insights.
- Developed recommendations to improve vehicle availability and fleet operations.

**Key findings:**
- Customer rentals accounted for 87.44% of analyzed rentals.
- Service-related rentals accounted for 12.56%.
- Average rental duration was approximately 38.44 minutes.

**Focus:** Operational analytics, KPI monitoring, SQL, data cleaning,
exploratory analysis, and business recommendations.
